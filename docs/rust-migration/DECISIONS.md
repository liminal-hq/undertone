# Decisions

The decision log is **append-only**: each answer is added as a new entry with its date, the answer, and what it changed. An earlier entry is never edited; a later entry supersedes it and says so. Questions still open are in the second table, with their defaults; when one is answered, add its entry to the log and mark the open row as answered.

The agent proceeds on the default for anything local and reversible, and **stops** before anything outward-facing (GitHub merges, tags, publishing, the live site, another repository) until the person has answered.
