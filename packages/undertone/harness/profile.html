Undertone worklet profile

Plays a ladder of small patterns through the AudioWorklet backend, one after another, and times a block of each. The differences between neighbouring steps show where a device spends its time: the fixed cost of a block with nothing playing, then the dry voices, then the reverb, then the delay. The numbers are sent to the machine that served this page. Keep this window in front and leave the device alone while it runs (about two minutes).
